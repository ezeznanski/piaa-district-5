October 6, 2026 · Golf

District V Crowns 2026 Golf Champions

District V recognized its 2026 golf champions following the District V Golf Championships.

In the boys' individual competition, Parker Oakman of McConnellsburg claimed the district championship. Ryder Shumaker of Somerset finished second, followed by Trenton Ripple of Bedford in third and Colton Boozer of Chestnut Ridge in fourth.

Olivia Nichols of Rockwood earned the girls' individual championship, with Kaydence Dufour of Chestnut Ridge finishing as the runner-up. Somerset captured the District V boys' team championship.

Congratulations to all of this year's District V golf champions and qualifiers.
