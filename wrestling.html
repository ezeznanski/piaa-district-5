2026–27 Postseason

Wrestling Championship Dates

Some dates, locations and regional formats remain subject to PIAA approval. Refer to the official packet for complete procedures.

Boys Team Wrestling

District V Dual Meet Championship

February 6, 2027 · Higher-seeded team hosts. At least six qualifying teams; field may expand to eight.

PIAA preliminary round: February 9 (site TBD). State team championships: February 12–13 at 1st Summit Arena, Johnstown.

Boys Individual Wrestling

District V & Southwest Regional

District championship: February 27 at North Star High School; weigh-ins at 9 a.m. and wrestling at 10 a.m.

Southwest Regional: March 5–6 at a District 7 site to be determined. Proposed regional format is subject to approval.

Girls Individual Wrestling

District V/IX & Central Regional

District V/IX championship: February 20 at Chestnut Ridge High School; weigh-ins at 9 a.m. and wrestling at 10 a.m.

Central Regional: March 6 at Milton High School; three combined District V/IX qualifiers.

PIAA Individual State Championships: March 11–13, 2027. Confirm the venue and operational details in the final PIAA schedule.
